Why we created Little Chef

For more than two decades, Pista House has made food enjoyed by families across generations. Behind every product is a world most children never get to see.

  • 🍞Bakers preparing dough
  • 👩‍🍳Chefs working together
  • 🔍Quality teams checking standards
  • ⚙️Machines making food consistently
  • 📦Packaging teams at work
  • 🏭Warehouses organising ingredients

Little Chef opens that world to children, so they see that food takes science, people, culture, hygiene, creativity and teamwork.

    Questions parents and schools ask

    Can’t find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us on +91 91333 08091.

    How long is the Little Chef experience?

    About 90 to 120 minutes, depending on the programme and group size.

    What age group can join?

    Little Chef is designed for children aged 4 to 14, with activities adapted to each age group.

    Is Little Chef only for schools?

    No. Schools are our main visitors, and families and private groups can request a booking too, subject to availability.

    How many students can a school bring?

    We recommend 25 to 40 students per visit, with one teacher for every 20 students.

    Will children enter working production areas?

    Children follow designated, guided routes. Access to production areas is controlled to meet safety and hygiene requirements.

    What does each child take home?

    An official Little Chef certificate, a Factory Passport, a special Pista House cookie box and a group photo memory.

    Is food provided?

    Yes. Students enjoy refreshments and a complimentary meal from Pista House. Please tell us about any allergies when you book.

    Can we take photographs?

    Photo rules vary by area. The Little Chef team will explain them during the visit, and every school group gets a group photo.
